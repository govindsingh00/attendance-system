Core Features

Everything your institution needs

A complete attendance management platform built for schools, colleges, and organizations of any size.

🧠

Face Recognition

ArcFace deep learning model identifies students in real time. No ID cards, no manual calls — just instant recognition.

👥

Role-Based Access

Separate dashboards for Admin, Teacher, and Student. Each role sees exactly what they need — nothing more.

📊

Attendance Reports

Filter records by date, course, section, or student. Spot absentee patterns and generate reports instantly.

🛡️

Anti-Proxy System

Session-based duplicate detection ensures no student can be marked present twice. Attendance fraud eliminated.

✏️

Manual Override

Teachers retain full control. Mark or correct attendance manually alongside the automated face recognition.

📱

Simple & Fast

How It Works

From camera to record in seconds

Simple for teachers, invisible for students. The system handles everything automatically.

1

Admin sets up the system

Admin registers students and teachers, creates courses, and assigns teachers to sections.

2

Student faces are enrolled

Each student's face is captured and trained into the recognition model. Done once, works forever.

3

Teacher starts attendance

Teacher opens the face attendance session. Students walk past the camera — recognized instantly.

4

Attendance saved automatically

Present/Absent status is saved to the database in real time. Students can view their own records anytime.
